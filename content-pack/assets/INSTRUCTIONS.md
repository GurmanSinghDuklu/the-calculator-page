## How to Convert to PNG/JPG (3 options)

### Option 1 — Easiest: Browser screenshot
1. Open the .svg file in Chrome or Safari (drag it in)
2. Right-click → "Save image as" → saves as PNG at full resolution

### Option 2 — Free online (best quality)
- Go to cloudconvert.com
- Upload the SVG → convert to PNG at 2x scale
- Download — will be 2400x1260px at perfect sharpness

### Option 3 — Mac Preview
1. Open SVG in Preview
2. File → Export → PNG or JPEG
3. Set resolution to 144 DPI for crisp output
